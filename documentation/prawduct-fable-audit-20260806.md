## Executive summary

**Overall verdict: the machine is sound; the growth model is the risk.** Across ~1,000 unreleased commits, the audit found no hard state dead-ends, no correctness rot in the core data plane, and a branch implementation that is coherent and unusually honest about its own limits. The recurring defect is not in any mechanism but in a repeated *shape*: surfaces whose claim is wider than their mechanism's jurisdiction — backstops gated off in reachable configurations, records that summarize away a narrowing a review fought for, prose triggers with no pin, nudges whose advice cannot discharge them, and always-loaded prompt text that restates rather than routes. Meanwhile every durable store (learnings, state, backlog, evidence, the digest) grows monotonically against controls that are measured, documented — and not yet bending the curve. The consumer fleet proves both halves: governance demonstrably lands and is heavily used where the plugin reaches. (~10 v1 file-sync repos receive nothing, but the owner treats those as abandoned projects — the live distribution concern is the **v3 fleet**, where updates are evergreen by design (`main` + `autoUpdate`) and the real breaks are a stuck backlog migration, an advisory that fails to auto-resolve, and whatever a dormant v3 repo hits on its first session back.)

**Q1 — Uplevel vs. enumerate: uplevel the validation layer, keep enumerating the semantics.** The code half of a domain stack is *already* upleveled — `advisory_store` is a generic probe registry, `probe_families` a one-roster composition root, and `coverage_probes` already governs seven artifact domains as data with zero per-domain prose (observability rides it with no dedicated probe, doctor check, or janitor theme). What stays hand-built is the prose half (elicitation, check semantics, spec) plus bespoke test sweeps — and the branch's own cost decomposition shows the expensive part (≈10 review rounds) went to domain-specific *semantic* defects no registry could generate or catch. Recommendation: generalize the `_POLICY_SURFACES` sweep harness and mechanically validate the cross-cutting-concerns registry's citations (cuts the largest mechanical cost of domain N+1, ~38% of inserted lines), and explicitly do **not** build prose-stack generation — it would flatten exactly the quantifiers the repo has learned transcription flattens, and the work-model review already killed this class of apparatus once.

**Q2 — Correctness: yes, with a named residual class.** The kernel-v3 review lifecycle closed its known dead-end classes *structurally* (refusals name remedies reachable from the refusing state; archived reviews restore; dormant pointers derive from the branch). The branch will very likely achieve goal 1 (policies get recorded — the advisory reaches every plugin-era vintage) while goal 2 (conformance) is prose-enforced by declared posture. The open class: **jurisdiction narrower than the claim** — the abandoned-review Stop backstop only fires in plan-governed sessions with visible changes; the verify-resolutions anchor is per-worktree while its facts are clone-shared (silently demoting the cheap remedy to a full round); the evidence store has a growth advisory but no compaction command; the Critic's dependency bullet was silently narrowed from "against no recorded policy *or against the recorded one*" to presence-only; and the repo's own one-line resolution fact still carries the tier-1 overclaim shape the cumulative review existed to remove.

**Q3 — Prompt style: modern in register, dated in architecture.** Caps-pressure language is nearly gone (0–1 per file; `pr/SKILL.md` is the one exception), rules carry rationale, and several house conventions (severity stated once; counts derived never persisted; "norms are prose read by capable models") independently converge on Claude-5 guidance. The gaps are structural: the same 4–6 rules restated in 4–6 places each (with the digest *designed* to restate CLAUDE.md, and `reflection.md:128` codifying "CLAUDE.md repetition" as an escalation arm — an older-model coping strategy now institutionalized); incident archaeology and migration-relative phrasing woven through instruction files; a digest pinned at ~98% of a hard 10KB gate with "anticipate the trim" authoring economics standing where progressive disclosure would shrink the always-loaded surface; and two monolith skills (doctor 36KB, backlog 31KB) beside one exemplary mode-routed skill (critic).

**Q4 — Exemplar: of mechanisms, yes; of proportionality, not yet — and it knows.** The framework dogfoods nearly everything it prescribes (all strategy artifacts, a ratified 26-norm registry with real enforcement rows, a recorded dependency policy with committed `constraints.txt`, reflection discipline that prices its own mistakes) and measures its own governance tax with exemplary instrumentation. But the measurements haven't changed the trajectory: ~10 reviews for a 6-chunk docs+config feature; `verify-resolutions` is half of all Critic reviews at the lowest actionability; learnings regrew +26KB in seven days after its third compaction while three governing surfaces give contradictory format instructions; two size nudges fire every session with no dischargeable action; `documentation/` has at least one structurally wrong doc and no staleness mechanism; and the framework runs with *less* backlog governance than its fleet (7 checks dormant on the Issues backend it pioneered).

**Characterization of the gaps** — four shapes account for nearly everything found:

1. **Jurisdiction gaps**: an invariant's guard covers fewer states than the invariant claims (Stop backstop, verify anchor, presence-vs-conformance, flat-fact summaries).
2. **Dead letters**: signals with no consumer or no dischargeable action (size nudges, dismissal-yield, evidence-growth advisory, unimplemented mode-collapse proposal).
3. **Repetition as enforcement**: rules restated across surfaces instead of homed once and routed — the prompt-architecture cost center, now self-replicating (the digest's growth section).
4. **Distribution debt**: improvements that never reach part of the fleet (a stuck backlog migration, an advisory that doesn't auto-resolve, un-exercised resume paths for dormant v3 repos; the v1 stragglers are abandoned by owner ruling and out of scope).

## Detailed findings

### 1. Enumerated domains vs. an upleveled approach

**The inventory.** ~12 governed domains at varying depth; the authoritative registry is `.prawduct/cross-cutting-concerns.md` (36 rows). Roster totals: 9 registered advisory probe families (`plugin/lib/probe_families.py:43-60`), 15 doctor checks, 11 janitor themes, ~10 discovery "Surface X" sections, 8 spec docs, 22 templates. The fullest stack (upstream deps, this branch) occupies ~19 distinct surfaces; the thinnest (observability) occupies essentially zero dedicated surfaces because it rides the generic coverage chain (`plugin/lib/coverage_probes.py:88-127`).

**What is already generic.** `advisory_store.py` (registration, lifecycle, reconcile/dismiss/retention, generic capability detectors); `probe_families.py` (2 lines to register a domain's ambient nudge); `coverage_probes.py`'s expectation tables (`UNIVERSAL_ARTIFACTS` / `TRIGGERED_ARTIFACTS` — seven artifact domains as data, single-homed, imported never transcribed); `coverage_algebra.py` (one generic gate question); the `_POLICY_SURFACES` roster mechanism (`tests/test_v5_templates.py:568-585` — data-driven sweep over 11 surfaces × 3 scope types, with scope *derived* from heading dedication rather than asserted).

**What the marginal domain actually costs.** This branch: 36 files, +3,190 lines, 6 chunks, ~15–18 review invocations, ~16 hours wall clock. Decomposition: test guards ~38%, templates ~12%, spec ~5%, probe ~4%, scattered methodology/skill prose only ~3% (~85 lines across 10 files). The enumeration across surfaces is *not* where the time went — the review rounds went to cross-surface semantic coherence defects (a pairing claim false of one of the pair; advisory-type literals uncoupled from `PROBE_TYPE`) that are invisible to any structural registry.

**The deliberate unevenness is policy, not drift.** Runbooks intentionally have no Critic check (`cross-cutting-concerns.md:48`); the descent obligation intentionally has no advisory (`:34`); the registry forbids automating its own validation-of-sufficiency (`:70`) while admitting its cells drift invisibly (`:72-77`). The work model's own history records an independent review killing a parallel governance apparatus for re-deriving machinery under new names (`documentation/work-model.md:36-55`), and the house style defers generic mechanisms until evidence seeds them (`work-model-spec.md:86-91`).

**Recommendation.**
- **Build**: a narrow `domain_registry` in the mold of `coverage_probes`' tables — each domain declares its surface roster (path/heading/scope), spec path, probe family, doctor/janitor/Critic anchors. Consumers: (a) a generalized surface-sweep harness lifted from `test_v5_templates.py:588-637`, replacing ~600 bespoke test lines per domain with a ~20-line roster entry; (b) registry-vs-reality checks (declared surface exists, heading resolves, citing line cites the spec) — closing the admitted `ccc.md:72-77` drift gap without violating the `:70` don't-automate-the-thinking rule; (c) anchor-presence validation replacing per-domain hand-pinned tests.
- **Do not build**: prose-stack generation (elicitation, check semantics, spec text from schema). Evidence says the prose is not schematic — check semantics genuinely differ per domain (presence vs. three-verdict conformance vs. marker-position vs. staged chain), the elicitation defaults-with-whys are the differentiated value, and generated elicitation is the hardcoded questionnaire `discovery.md:117` explicitly rejects. Revisit only after two more domains demonstrate the prose is actually parallel.

### 2. Correctness of what was implemented

#### 2a. The upstream-dependency-policy branch

**Verdict: correct, coherent, honest about limits; goal 1 (recording) well-served, goal 2 (conformance) prose-enforced by declared posture.** The advisory fires universally on the missing fact with no detection gate, so already-built plugin-era products are genuinely reached (verified in a fixture per the plan's Chunk 05 record). Branch governance is closed: `check-cumulative-critic` reports `satisfied`, 0 unresolved blocking; 138 policy-related tests pass; follow-ups #608/#609 open.

Material findings (medium severity unless noted):

1. **The Critic leg was silently narrowed.** Requirements §7 (`.prawduct/artifacts/upstream-dependency-policy-discovery.md:227`) commit the Critic to flag a dependency change "against no recorded policy **or against the recorded one**"; the delivered bullets (`plugin/skills/critic/review-protocol.md:73`, `goals-1-3.md:76`) check presence only. A 2-day-old release adopted under a 7-day policy passes review unremarked. The narrowing happened at plan-authoring with no recorded descope — the same requirement-dropped-by-a-pivot class the cumulative review caught elsewhere (R-1/R-18) and missed here.
2. **"Setting the policy triggers the scan" is one unpinned prose sentence** (`discovery.md:163`). Both documented resolution routes do reach the scan, but no test pins the sentence, and an owner who records the policy by editing YAML directly never meets it — `surfaces` then gets authored from memory, the exact under-enumeration the design exists to prevent, with no state distinguishing a scanned record from a remembered one.
3. **Ambient resolution is self-attested.** The probe suppresses on the truthy flat fact alone (`dependency_policy_probes.py:82`); "decided in name only" (fact set, block null) goes permanently silent ambiently. Doctor #15(a) names the state, but doctor is on-demand and nothing routes anyone there once the nudge is quiet.
4. **Tier-3-must-be-a-procedure is enforced nowhere, and prawduct itself is in the prompt state** (`docs/upstream-dependency-policy.md:95-108` vs. `.prawduct/artifacts/security-model.md:188-194`). Honest per the tier rules, but the clause-1 age floor outside CI is enforced by agent memory plus one digest line, fleet-wide, permanently.
5. **The repo's own flat fact still overstates tier 1** (`.prawduct/project-state.yaml:612` omits the local/agent tier-3 row the R-2/R-16 fix added to the detailed records) — low-medium, but it is the committed one-line summary a skimming reader meets first.
6. **Tier 1 is recorded as *reached* but CI has never run** — the branch is unpushed; the closure guard's design depends on the 3.10 CI leg; the caveat lives only in gitignored handoff notes. **Push before merge and let CI be the verification the records claim.**
7. **Loose residuals**: three ride-along items (a false "one test class each" docstring claim, a second repo-local "7-day" copy at `constraints.txt:61`, the stale `tests.yml:27` comment) live only in gitignored handoff files — the exact "filed lesson is a repeated lesson" carrier the framework distrusts.

No hard dead-ends; what exists are **silent parking orbits** — states with a documented exit that nothing routes you toward (fact-without-block; dismissed-advisory-plus-doctor-never-run; recorded-but-never-scanned; tier-3-without-procedure).

#### 2b. The develop-side machinery

**Verdict: mostly class-closed; the strongest engineering in the repo.** The review lifecycle's known dead-ends were fixed at the class level: refusals choose remedies *from on-disk state* (`active_dispatch_refusal`, `restore_refusal`); `critic-restore` is a full inverse operation with pre-flight consolidatability; scope derives from the branch at every consumer; review-identity keying (#605) closed straggler misattribution structurally; consolidation is idempotent and fail-closed; the shared evidence store uses single-syscall appends with read-side dedupe and excludes corrupt lines in the safe direction.

Remaining hazards, all one class — **backstops and anchors whose jurisdiction is narrower than the invariant they claim**:

1. **Abandoned-review Stop backstop gated behind `has_changes && has_build_plan`** (`plugin/bin/prawduct-hook:1836-1843`): a dispatched review on a clean tree or plan-less branch whose coordinator dies is silently swept; recorded-but-unconsolidated findings sit until a future dispatch trips over them. Medium.
2. **Verify-resolutions anchor is per-worktree; facts are clone-shared** (`critic_consolidate.py:1158-1201`; self-documented at `coverage_algebra.py:300-311`): from a fresh worktree the gate prescribes the cheap delta pass, the anchor is missing, and the skill silently demotes to a full review. Medium — the advice and the reachable remedy diverge.
3. **Evidence store: unbounded growth, measured trigger, no implemented remedy** (`evidence.py:563,661-667`): the 10,000-tree advisory recommends compaction; no compaction command exists. Gate cost is linear in distinct trees and paid at every session end, forever. Medium, slow-burn.
4. **TOCTOU window in the concurrent-dispatch guard** (marker written by the CLI seconds after `begin_review` checks it; a racing dispatch displaces the first, and `_archive_leftovers` can file a foreign straggler under the wrong review id, fragmenting restore). Low-medium.
5. **`waived` resolutions lift blocking findings clone-wide, forever, on a model's say-so guarded only by a directive** (`coverage_algebra.py:126-146` vs. the structurally-safe builder-side `accept` that *cannot* unblock). The one place the "no model in the write path" doctrine bends. Medium by design-tension.
6. Minor: archive cap silently deletes the 4th displaced review; the 15-minute in-flight grace and the 30-minute marker TTL are two different definitions of "dead" that disagree for 15 minutes; `cost-of-commit` over-prices the verbatim post-review commit (safe direction, but wrong in exactly the most common case, which taxes trust in an advisory whose only value is being trusted).

**Gate economics: proportionate by design now, and the design shows its work.** The docstring-fix incident (satisfied → two test-docstring fixes → uncovered → full round) is priced-in deliberately (`coverage_algebra.py:75-89` rules out content-equivalence carve-outs because this repo reads source as text); today's re-cover cost for that same incident is one delta verify pass, advertised before the commit. Dispositions structurally cannot weaken gates; "accept real defects to stay green" is only possible for findings that never held the gate. The residual pressure point is (5): a builder who wants green has an incentive to steer the verify reviewer toward `waived`.

### 3. Prompt-side style vs. Claude-5-generation practice

**Quantitative shape.** Caps-pressure is nearly absent (0–1 per file; `pr/SKILL.md` has 8 including a `CRITICAL:` banner from 2026-03). Soft-register prohibition density is real: ~1 "never" per 200–250 words in the large files (`review-cycle.md` 30, doctor 27, norms 25, backlog 23), almost always with rationale — better than bare walls, still a constraint volume Claude-5 models don't need. Always-loaded: product sessions ≈12–15KB of framework text (10.1KB digest pinned against a 10,000-char code gate + ~1.1KB anchor + briefing); framework sessions ≈22KB. On-demand extremes: critic `final` ≈89KB across four files (well-routed); doctor 36KB and backlog 31KB as monoliths.

**Findings by pattern** (evidence spot-samples):

- **Incident archaeology in instruction files** — the highest-volume defect. "Observed live: four rounds and ~40 minutes of review on a ~40-line code change" (`review-cycle.md:274-276`); "missed its 1-2 min target in 30 of 30 recorded runs" (`critic/SKILL.md:51` — false the day run 31 lands); issue IDs and finding IDs in always-loaded prose (`CRT-3X9D` in CLAUDE.md). Rule authority should be the behavior prescribed, not the incident; narratives already have homes (learnings, CHANGELOG). Keep the one actionable tool-contract workaround (`anthropics/claude-code#34164`).
- **Migration-relative phrasing** — text written as a diff against a prompt version the model never saw: "Prawduct **no longer** selects a reviewer model… intelligent model switching **has been removed**" (`pr/SKILL.md:94`); "The Enforcement norm index **used to ship** two illustrative rows…" (`doctor/SKILL.md:60`); "the severity contract no longer says 'recommend backlog' anywhere, and reviewers must not reintroduce it" (`review-cycle.md:265` — instructing the model not to restore text it never saw). Rewrite present-tense, state-based.
- **Institutionalized redundancy.** The standing close block is stated in near-full form 4× (`reflection.md:65-91` canonical, `building.md:120-133`, both digests); "STOP and read building.md" 5×; merge-commit mechanics 4×; tests-are-contracts 5×. The slim digest already proves the fix — its header says "carries only what CLAUDE.md does not restate" — but the product digest has no such subtraction rule against the anchor. Deepest conflict: `reflection.md:128` names "CLAUDE.md repetition" as a legitimate enforcement escalation arm — repetition-as-enforcement is the older-model coping strategy, codified. Recurrence should escalate to a hook check or Critic rule (code), which the framework itself names as the stronger home.
- **Budget economics vs. progressive disclosure.** The digest sits at 10,145 bytes against a 10,000-char gate with a test-enforced ceiling and "anticipate the trim" authoring guidance (`planning.md:91`); the handoff records digest headroom of 10 characters. A file permanently at 98% of a hard gate is the signal that content is competing for a fixed always-on slot instead of moving to the surface that owns the moment.
- **Choreography for judgment tasks** (contained): backlog pick-scoring arithmetic and worked query-parsing examples (`backlog/SKILL.md:108-126`); doctor's "~6 candidates → flat dump banned" numeric rule. The principles beneath each are one sentence. Keep, by contrast, every data-plane sequence (critic steps 1–7, exit codes, marker lifecycle) — that is fragile-operation scripting done right.

**What already aligns — keep and extend**: the calm register with rationale; principles-as-principles (the 24-roster); the critic skill's mode-routed protocol split ("a three-goal review pays a seven-goal reading fee"); fork-context isolation for heavy skills; prose-rules-made-code (digest gate, record-lint pre-computation, fail-closed consolidation); "prefer an invariant to a tally in durable prose"; severity stated once and generally; norms.md's Deliberate Non-Design section — "Norms are prose read by capable models… Structure would trade away the judgment this depends on" is the Claude-5 thesis stated in the repo's own voice, months before the guidance post.

### 4. Self-hosting: exemplar or not

**Exemplar of mechanisms.** All strategy artifacts present; ratified 26-norm registry with per-row mechanism/audit-home (and the candid record that "CI (test)" was fiction until 2026-08-04); recorded dependency policy with committed `constraints.txt` whose header explains its own closure; 0 attribution trailers and true merge commits against harness defaults; reflection discipline that prices its own mistakes and demonstrably changes behavior within the hour (the branch's closing reflection paying one avoidable round, then declining the next for a smaller reason).

**Not yet an exemplar of proportionality — with its own instrumentation as witness.**

- **Governance tax, measured and standing**: 535 review events totaling ~56 hours; 2,289 findings at 59% actionable; `verify-resolutions` is 50% of Critic reviews at 21–55% actionable; 10 reviews for this branch's 6-chunk docs+config feature. The July efficiency review's verdict ("the overbuild is in everything wrapped around that core"; ~88% of the repo's own backlog is governance self-maintenance) remains accurate a month later; its mode-collapse proposal (4→2) is unimplemented with all four modes in live use.
- **The learning loop is drowning in its own success**: learnings regrew 34KB→60KB in seven days after its third compaction; 197 rules; the per-entry lint grandfathers all existing entries; **2 of 197** entries carry any lifecycle metadata, so the promotion/staleness tracker is inert; and three governing surfaces now contradict each other on entry format (`reflection.md:134` and the briefing nudge demand one-line rules with narrative in `learnings-detail.md`; the corpus preamble at `learnings.md:18-22` asserts the opposite). Nothing measures whether learnings are ever recalled.
- **Dead-letter nudges**: the project-state size warning prescribes three compactions the file has already done (0 acceptance-criteria blocks, 8 change-log entries); it and the learnings nudge fire every session with no dischargeable action — the exact "noise that trains dismissal" the repo's own briefing code warns about at `briefing.py:768-771`.
- **Living Documentation fails in `documentation/`** with no covering mechanism: `project-structure.md` diagrams a pre-plugin-move layout and says marketplace publish is "pending" at v3.2.6 — *after* an Aug 2 drift-burndown commit touched it; two more docs are frozen drafts. `record_lint` covers `.prawduct/` only; the janitor's Documentation Fitness theme is on-demand and evidently didn't reach it.
- **The pioneer's penalty**: on the Issues backlog backend, 7 backlog checks are dormant — the framework currently receives less backlog oversight than any markdown-backend product — while its open count grew ~3.5× in a month (50 → 175, partly migration normalization). The one pending incoming bug (the discodon import blocker) has sat un-triaged for four sessions against CLAUDE.md's own triage instruction.
- **Divergences from what products get** are mostly deliberate and sound (slim digest with fail-safe; live-tree plugin as true dogfooding). Two worth deciding consciously: the framework's committed `.claude/settings.json` is `{}` (a fresh clone carries no governance bootstrap — the one artifact it insists products commit), and the framework is always governed by unreleased, possibly mid-feature gate code no product experiences.
